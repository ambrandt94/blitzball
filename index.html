Mouse look (right/up) · click captures · Esc releases
E claim · while squared up, E toggles catch-ready · Q drop · hold F pass, or kick if you are squared up with the ball
With the ball, hold LMB to throw. The aim locks to you and the ball stays in hand. Meter, Hold, and Tap: the mouse bends the arc and the marker slides with power. Target: mouse left/right bends, mouse up/down (or the wheel) sets where it lands. Pull: drag down to charge, sideways to bend. RMB cancels, including the tap window.
Without the ball, tap LMB for a short shoulder check. Hold it to charge a power tackle (the bar by the reticle fills in about half a second) and release to lunge. While catch-ready, that click deflects a nearby ball along your aim and does not catch it. The click that captures the mouse does not check. Taps during a tap-power window do not check.
Hold RMB to square up. The body treads upright, with or without the ball, and swim speed drops. Without the ball, E toggles catch-ready: both hands reach, a soft pass in the cone is caught, and a very hard shot can fumble. Letting go of RMB drops the stance. With the ball, hold F to drop-kick: same aim marker and power mode as a throw, then the ball leaves the foot. Releasing RMB cancels the kick. Hold F with empty hands and swim into a loose ball to volley it. The aqua beacon is where a loose ball will settle.
G call for a pass. The roster match menu sets 1v1 through 5v5. Field bots pass and shoot; goalies and test chasers stay on their own brains. A hand edit becomes Custom. Goalie skill and field AI skill are in the Teams section.
