With the ball, hold LMB to throw. The aim direction locks to you, the ball stays in hand, and the landing marker slides with power and drifts with you. A click with no mouse movement throws straight. Release throws in Meter and Hold. Tap mode: release, then click to add power.
Without the ball, LMB shoulder-checks along your swim (or your facing, if you are not steering). It knocks swimmers back and pops a held ball loose. The click that captures the mouse does not check. Taps during a tap-power window do not check.
Hold RMB off-ball to reach and catch (wider mitt, no aim mark). With the ball, RMB puts the landing marker on the straight throw and shows no curve. The number on the marker is the landing distance. Faint rings mark min and max reach while you throw. The aqua beacon is where a loose ball will settle.
G call for a pass. With Test opponent on, one orange swimmer chases slowly and just holds the ball. Turn it off for the green teammate; orange then holds unless Defense chases is on.
MMB toggles wide view · V zoom · ` Dev (invert look) · R reset ball
Gamepad: left stick move (while RT is held it creeps and the right stick bends the arc), RB/LT up/down, A claim, X pass, RT shoot, Y aim, LB zoom
